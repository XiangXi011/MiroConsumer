# MiroConsumer GTM Strategy

## Positioning

MiroConsumer sits between academic agent simulation and commercial consumer insights tools. The wedge is faster directional learning before teams spend budget on panels, surveys, or launch-scale experiments.

## Ideal Customer Profile

The initial ideal customer profile is a team that repeatedly tests new consumer propositions and has enough research budget to value speed, auditability, and repeatability.

Primary segments:

- consumer insights teams in FMCG, retail, food, beverage, beauty, and consumer health
- innovation agencies that run concept, copy, packaging, price, and A/B studies for multiple brands
- growth and brand strategy teams that need pre-launch risk signals before commissioning larger studies
- enterprise R&D or digital labs that require tenant isolation, private deployment, and audit trails

The strongest buyer has a recurring backlog of concepts and a painful delay between idea creation and first consumer feedback.

## Buyer Roles

The review owners should be identified early in the sales process:

- Business owner: VP Insights, Head of Innovation, Brand Strategy Lead
- Technical owner: data platform, security, or enterprise architecture reviewer
- Finance owner: procurement or budget holder validating per-run economics
- Daily champion: research manager or agency strategist who prepares briefs and interprets reports

## Sales Channels

Recommended sales channels:

- founder-led enterprise pilots for the first lighthouse accounts
- agency partnerships for packaged concept-testing programs
- product-led sandbox for low-risk Professional trials
- account-based outbound to innovation and consumer insights leaders
- marketplace or cloud partner listing after security and procurement assets mature

## Competitive Pricing

The competitive pricing approach should be benchmarked against three alternatives:

- traditional survey or panel vendors, usually slower but stronger on real respondent validity
- synthetic research and AI persona tools, often faster but weaker on audit trails and evidence binding
- strategy or innovation agencies, higher service depth but less repeatable software margin

MiroConsumer should avoid underpricing against generic AI chat tools. The paid value is structured simulation, repeatable Persona Packs, evidence-bound reports, tenant controls, and explainable diagnostics.

## Offer Packaging

Professional offer:

- shared SaaS workspace
- standard Persona Packs
- API access
- 16 consumers, 32 consumers, or 64 consumers per run
- standard report and export quota

Enterprise offer:

- private deployment option
- custom Persona Pack
- dedicated resource quota
- SSO/security review support
- 128 consumers tier
- audit export, SBOM, and operational evidence

## Pilot Motion

A pilot should run for four to six weeks:

- Week 1: select two real historical briefs and define success criteria.
- Week 2: configure Persona Pack and run baseline simulations.
- Week 3: compare outputs against past research or expert judgment.
- Week 4: run one live brief and assess workflow fit.
- Week 5-6: decide edition, quota, deployment model, and renewal path.

## Commercial Guardrails

Sales material must state that MiroConsumer provides directional research simulation. It must not claim statistically representative survey results, guaranteed sales lift, medical advice, investment advice, or legal advice.
