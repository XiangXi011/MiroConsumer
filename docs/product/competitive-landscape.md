# Competitive Landscape

MiroConsumer sits at the intersection of a 学术agent simulation工具 and a
商业消费者洞察工具.

## Positioning

- **Academic agent simulation tool**: the runtime models consumer agents,
  propagation, evidence chains, and replayable interventions.
- **Commercial consumer insight tool**: the report layer turns simulations into
  structured outputs that product, research, and growth teams can act on.

## Competitive Matrix

| Product | Agent simulation | Dynamic propagation | Explainable evidence chain | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MiroConsumer | Yes | Yes | Yes | Combines simulation, report traceability, and business workflow. |
| Remesh | Partial | Limited | Partial | Strong live discussion workflow, less emphasis on propagation modeling. |
| Zappi | No | No | Partial | Fast concept testing, but mostly static scoring and survey logic. |
| Aytm | No | No | Partial | Useful for research ops, not agent-based diffusion modeling. |
| NetLogo | Yes | Yes | No | Powerful simulation engine, but not packaged for consumer insight delivery. |
| AgentPy | Yes | Yes | No | Flexible simulation framework, but not opinionated for business reporting. |

## Differentiation

1. **Static scorecard vs dynamic propagation**  
   MiroConsumer emphasizes 动态传播 and round-based change, not only a single
   survey score.

2. **Black-box score vs explainable evidence chain**  
   Findings carry a 可解释证据链: source quality, evidence validation, and
   confidence metadata.

3. **Research prototype vs business workflow**  
   The product can be used as a simulation tool and as a consumer-insight tool
   without changing the core contract.

## Notes for readers

This matrix is intentionally conservative. It focuses on the dimensions that
matter most for consumer propagation testing: agent simulation, dynamic spread,
and explainable output.
